# Hangouts Zero: what the MIT license covers

Covered (Zero.LICENSE): the Zero backend, meaning everything registered on `window.Hangouts`
(router, storage, profile, package, player, catalog and home feed cores), the game SDK,
`Zero.Map.json` and `Zero.Lexicon.json`.

Not covered: the sidebar and every other user interface, its styles, icons and embedded fonts
(Hangouts One, the launcher and Hangouts Online Services are proprietary).

Third-party parts in the file (the embedded font and the Gravity UI icon sprite) keep their own licenses.
Check both before publishing the backend on its own.
